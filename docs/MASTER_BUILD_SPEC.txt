15. SOURCE CONFLICT RESOLUTION
======================================================================

Different providers will sometimes disagree.

Rivexis must not silently choose one value.

Create:

SourceConflict

Fields:

metric
source_a
value_a
source_b
value_b
difference
difference_percentage
expected_tolerance
severity
resolution_method
resolved_value
resolution_confidence

Resolution policy:

1. Determine whether providers measure the same concept.
2. Normalize units and timestamps.
3. Compare observation time.
4. Prefer primary/authoritative source for the metric.
5. Preserve alternative values.
6. Reduce confidence when unexplained divergence remains.
7. Expose material conflicts in evidence.

Example:

Provider A TVL = $1.02B
Provider B TVL = $970M

Do NOT immediately call one wrong.

Possible causes:

different block
different methodology
different pool inclusion
different asset valuation
different timestamp

Rivexis must understand this.

25. FINAL DECISION STATES
======================================================================

PROCEED

Conditions may include:

manageable detected risk
no critical blocker
adequate evidence
adequate confidence
policy compliance

MODIFY

Examples:

reduce approval
reduce position
change bridge
change route
add collateral
reduce leverage
change protocol
diversify stablecoin exposure
reduce concentration
adjust slippage

WAIT

Temporary risk or unreliable current state.

Examples:

network instability
oracle disruption
rapid market movement
liquidity deterioration
provider disagreement
unresolved security incident
stale state
pending governance event

AVOID

Material risk exceeds acceptable limits.

Examples:

known malicious destination
active exploit
critical protocol compromise
drainer behavior
dangerous approval
bridge compromise
extreme liquidation exposure
policy-prohibited dependency

UNKNOWN

Evidence is insufficient or contradictory.

Examples:

required provider unavailable
unsupported blockchain
unverified contract
unresolvable data conflict
insufficient protocol data
unreliable oracle information

UNKNOWN is a legitimate institutional outcome.

Do not hide uncertainty.
