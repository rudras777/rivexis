## Original concept

A single original sculptural risk landscape: layered matte graphite surfaces, fine imperial-teal contours, sparse exposure nodes, a champagne boundary and deliberate alternative pathways. Scene chapters follow exposure → position sensitivity → transaction constraints → capital alternatives → evidence. Geometry is conceptual, never a safety score, live position or source of financial outputs. Readable DOM content and canonical-model public examples remain authoritative and clearly illustrative.

## Technology evaluation

[Three.js](https://threejs.org/) and [React Three Fiber](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/getting-started/introduction) suit complex scenes and richer model assets; they add dependencies and runtime weight for a single procedural mesh. [GSAP](https://gsap.com/), [Motion](https://motion.dev/) and [Lenis](https://lenis.dev/) overlap with native transitions/scroll observers for this bounded choreography; scroll replacement is unnecessary. [Rive](https://rive.app/docs/runtimes/web/web-js) is suitable for authored2D state machines but adds an asset/runtime pipeline and does not establish a genuine3D financial surface.

Preferred prototype: lazy-loaded native WebGL with original procedural geometry and small shaders; CSS/SVG for the shared atmospheric background and accessible fallback. No new dependencies, remote models, paid assets or graphics services. Follow [WebGL best practices](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API/WebGL_best_practices): bounded pixel density/draw calls, reusable buffers, visibility-aware rendering, cleanup and context-loss fallback. No post-processing or high-intensity bloom.
